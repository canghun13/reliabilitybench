## Five deep checks

Scoring (judgment only): demand/long-tail30 + free gap20 + independent jobs20 +
repeat10 + feasibility10 + differentiation5 + fit/safety/maintenance5. A high

### 1. Anilox condition and ink transfer — 74 = 26+7+16+9+8+4+4

Genuine repeating jobs: check engraving volume, verify across-face uniformity,
plan ink for a run, compare post-clean recovery on the same measured locations.
Supplier evidence is unusually specific: Troika's public geometry and
consistency forms are visible and its [ink estimator](https://troika-systems.com/calculators/ink-consumption-estimator/)
already accepts print area/coverage and user-declared transfer. Commercial
instrument QC and database are distinct from those public forms. Cleaning
interest is visible; a local paired pre/post scan could be useful, but apparent
recovery needs the same locations, measurement method and declared tolerance.
It is not a second uniformity dashboard named differently. Even crediting that
one hole, three practical visits already have exact/strong alternatives. **Free
suite differentiation fails; narrow cleaning-recovery keyword lead only.**

### 2. Yarn evenness and defects — 75 = 23+14+14+9+7+4+4

CVm from equal-distance mass, normalized defect counts, periodic wavelength
evidence and optical hairiness are genuinely different *measurements*. They
aren't four browser tools if the accessible inputs are only one mass CSV.
[Uster](https://www.uster.com/products/staple-yarn-testing/uster-tester/) and
[Loepfe manual](https://www.loepfe.com/public/loepfe/Components/YM-3N1/ym_3n1_instructionmanual_frs3000_46360003_en.pdf)
show integrated hardware outputs; neither is mislabeled a free browser suite.
Narrow promising query: periodic peak wavelength from an existing length-indexed
export. No verified public export contract with enough comparable data for
optical hairiness or vendor benchmark equivalence; CV and normalized defect
counts also need test length/segmentation/threshold conditions. **Four
defensible independent browser jobs and benchmark source support unresolved.**

### 3. Shot-peening verification — 71 = 24+8+14+9+8+4+4

Repeat per approved setup/lot: intensity from strip curve, independent
image-based coverage, sieve distribution against an input spec, media/run
traceability. The last is record quality, not inherently a new fourth numerical
tool. [PeenSolver](https://electronics-inc.com/introducing-peensolver-free-curve-solver-web-app/)
expressly offers a free browser solver; Kirk's free requested Excel add-in is
separate and his site also lists coverage and two-strip tools. Browser-only
curve fitting is therefore not white space. Coverage cannot be inferred from
intensity alone; media suitability and approval depend on actual spec and
measurements. **Free-gap/independence fail for suite; no process sign-off.**

### 4. Measured sample color QC — 72 = 24+8+14+9+9+4+4

Different candidate tasks: Lab tolerance against a standard, spectrum→Lab,
paired metamerism under changed illuminants, lot drift after conditioning.
[ICC/CIE data](https://registry.color.org/colorimetry-data/) show why wavelength
grids/observers matter. Exact browser ΔE and reflectance calculators exist;
paired illuminant comparison also appears in [a specialist Metamerism Lab](https://ironoshirube.com/research/metamerism-lab/?from=iro_color&source_color=71),
indexed description only because the direct reader did not expose its body.
[Colourwise](https://colourwise.app/measurement/spectral-explorer) shows sample
reflectance and illuminant/observer outputs, not proof of arbitrary CSV batch
metamerism. A B2B multi-sample import could still be a narrow gap; splitting one
pair by illuminant is not independent and generic lot SPC is not a fourth color
physics job. **Differentiated breadth unproven despite good static feasibility.**

### 5. Microelectronics bond-test evidence — 74 = 23+14+14+9+6+4+4

Wire pull, ball shear and die shear actually load different features; preserve
failure-mode labels, geometry, units and test method. A fourth batch SPC view
may be helpful, but it is generic analysis of the same force records, not a
fourth new test decision. [Nordson Paragon](https://www.nordson.com/en/Products/Test-and-Inspection-Products/DAGE-Paragon-Software)
is instrument software; [Xyztec technical methods](https://www.xyztec.com/ja/knowledgecenter/guidelines-ja/mil-std-883-method-2011-9-bond-strength-destructive-bond-pull-test/)
and government method references establish technical long tails, not volume.
Narrow opportunity: local normalization of already released, comparable test
exports. Open cross-vendor schema and authoritative application limits not
verified; joint acceptance cannot come from force alone. **Fourth independent
job and evidence/maintenance gates unresolved.**
