### Five deep validations and hard gates

Scores: demand/long-tail30,free gap20,independent breadth20,repeat-use10,static
feasibility10,differentiation5,maintenance/safety/fit5. Editorial,not KD or tool
correctness. High totals cannot override fatal gates.

**Mechanical excitation preparation —80 =26+15+15+9+8+5+2.** Test engineers
repeat at PSD/pulse,fixture or payload changes. Strong tasks: integrate declared
PSD breakpoints and compare exposure; compute SRS from a sampled acceleration
history under a stated damping/solver; check classical excitation motion/force
against a declared actuator envelope. Units,PSD slopes,one/two-sided convention,
sampling/tail duration and damping must be explicit. Estimates cannot authorize
a qualification test or certify the test article/shaker safe.

KasperCalc already exposes breakpoint Grms and velocity/displacement estimates;
these outputs are not three tools. Crystal Instruments describes random,sine,
shock sizing in an app,not an arbitrary-history browser SRS service. MathWorks
and Vibrationdata provide genuine SRS methods/code alternatives,not proof that
every exact free-browser task is solved. A narrow local SRS gap is plausible.
However pulse motion and shaker capacity share one excitation-feasibility visit,
and a proposed fourth mount/transmissibility calculator repeats the old isolation
family. **D/H fail for the four-tool proposal**,not because own GSC is small.
Source/fixture work would be required for an actual implementation; no forced
fourth waveform/unit screen. **NO-GO.**

**PLC property verification —79 =25+16+13+9+7+5+4.** Controls engineers repeat
at logic revisions. The concrete task is evidence for/against a declared state
property under explicit input/scan assumptions. Useful outputs are a witness,
counterexample trace or a bounded/inconclusive result. Inputs need initialization,
retained state,input nondeterminism,function-block and timer semantics. A visual
simulator executing selected inputs cannot establish exhaustive safety.

TryPLC already exposes browser scan/debug behavior. ESBMC-PLC is a separate
