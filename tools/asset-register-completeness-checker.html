Method and decision boundary

Completeness = arithmetic mean of six field completion percentages

Every field has equal weight in this screening method. A populated value can still be wrong, stale, or inconsistent, so follow the ranked gaps with duplicate checks and source-document sampling.

When to use it

Use it before KPI reporting, a CMMS migration, a hierarchy rebuild, or a monthly data-quality review. Preserve the population definition and field rules when comparing periods.

Limitations

The six fields are a practical maintenance baseline, not a universal standard or compliance checklist. Add organization-specific fields outside this score and approve the data dictionary locally.
