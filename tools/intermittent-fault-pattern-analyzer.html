What the screen counts

Each event contributes once to its unit, phase, code, and recovery counts. An exact repeated signature requires the same phase, code, and recovery text after trimming. The leading-signature share is its count divided by all entered events.

What it cannot establish

Raw occurrence counts do not measure operating exposure. A startup signature may lead because the fleet performs more starts, and spelling differences may split one real category. Normalize with eligible operating hours, starts, cycles, environmental exposure, or another defensible denominator before comparing rates.
