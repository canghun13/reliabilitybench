Five different decisions, one controlled workflow.

The tools do not split one score across pages. The censoring audit decides whether sales history represents demand; classification describes the demand structure; forecasting creates candidate baselines; backtesting evaluates unseen performance; FVA audits a later human process after actuals exist.

Do not convert a forecast directly into a stocking verdict.

Intermittent-demand methods estimate a demand level. Stocking also needs lead-time uncertainty, consequence, repairability, installed base, substitutions, MOQ, shelf life, budget, and local service policy. Continue with the existing MRO planning tools only after the demand evidence is fit for use.
